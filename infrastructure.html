Field notes from the build

Infrastructure is a promise maintained by people.

Every layer below is physical equipment, operational work, shared rules, or a combination of all three.

01 / PHYSICAL

A cable is a route.

Fiber, copper, radio, and satellites move energy across distance. Without a physical or electromagnetic path, there is no conversation to abstract.

02 / LOGICAL

A switch is a decision point.

Routers and switches decide where traffic should go next. They create shared infrastructure by giving many flows a way through the same landscape.

03 / HUMAN

Standards make strangers interoperable.

Protocol documents, naming systems, policies, maintenance crews, and operators turn isolated links into a network people can depend on.

The point of the whole build

TCP is a protocol made possible by infrastructure.

Once links, switches, addresses, routes, interconnection, physical reach, and operations exist, TCP has a network to coordinate. It does not create that foundation; it makes a promise on top of it.
